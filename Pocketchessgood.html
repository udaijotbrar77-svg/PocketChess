About, privacy and credits
Privacy. Pocket Chess has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your games, ratings and settings stay on this device. Online features (the chess AI searching Google or Wikipedia, and playing a friend) only run when you choose them, and send only what is needed for that action, such as your question text. Online games use a free public service (PeerJS) to connect the two devices, then send moves directly between them. Group names are saved on your device and sent to your friend in that game.

Credits. Game, chess engine, openings, endgames and puzzles were written for this app with the help of AI. Wikipedia extracts shown by the lookup belong to Wikipedia contributors under CC BY-SA. The mascot artwork was made with an AI image tool for this project.
